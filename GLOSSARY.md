# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund request**:
A learner's ask to get their money back for a purchase, with their reason, waiting for someone on the team to review it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel purchase, reversal, chargeback

**Refund**:
Money returned to the learner's card after the team approves a refund request.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback

**Refund window**:
The 14 calendar days after a purchase during which the learner may submit a refund request.
_Avoid_: Cancellation period, cooling-off period

**Chargeback**:
A payment the learner's bank forcibly takes back through a card dispute, without our agreement. Never a synonym for refund.
